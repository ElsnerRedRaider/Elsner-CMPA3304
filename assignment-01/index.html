Top 5 Anime

A ranked list of my five favorite anime series.

  1. Dragon Ball Z

    The series that set the template for the modern shonen genre. Escalating power levels, iconic villains, and training arcs that defined how a generation thinks about anime.

  2. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

    A near-perfect story about brotherhood, sacrifice, and consequence. Tightly plotted from start to finish with one of the best endings in the medium.

  3. One Piece

    An enormous, ongoing world built one arc at a time. The payoff for long-term investment in these characters is unmatched anywhere else.

  4. Jujutsu Kaisen

    Sharp fight choreography and a cursed-energy system that stays clever instead of getting bloated. Modern shonen done with real style.

  5. Bleach

    Soul Society, Zanpakuto, and some of the best character designs in anime. The Thousand-Year Blood War arc closed it out in style.
