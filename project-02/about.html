About me

I'm a Computing Applications student at Texas Tech, dedicated to building quality websites and applications across a variety of use cases.

Why I started building

I got into this through a real problem. I needed a complete inventory of everything in a home, and doing it by hand would have taken days, so I built a prototype app to remove some of the legwork. That app became HomeProof.

Building HomeProof was the launchpad for my journey into web development. Since HomeProof I've built app prototypes, responsive websites through my coursework, and even a site for a local business.
