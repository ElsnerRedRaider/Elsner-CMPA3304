About

SHEET 01

I'm a Leadership Studies student at Texas Tech University, concentrating in Integrative Studies, Organizational Development, and Computing Applications. My path here wasn't a straight line — I started at UNT, changed majors more than once, spent time studying recording arts, worked my way through the apartment industry and door-to-door sales, and eventually landed in B2B software and cloud sales, where I work today as a Partner Account Manager. Coming back to finish the degree I didn't complete the first time around felt like the right next step.

Outside of the day job, I'm building Elsner App Development Services, a small software studio whose flagship product, HomeProof, helps people prove what they own when it matters most. I'm also a father of two daughters, a long-time guitar player working on my own songwriting, and — as you'll see below — a devoted Kevin Smith fan.
